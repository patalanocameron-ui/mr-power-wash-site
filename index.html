FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and cleaning solutions to kill mold, mildew and algae at the root. It's the safe way to clean roofs, stucco, siding and screens. Pressure washing uses high pressure and is best for hard surfaces like concrete driveways, sidewalks and pavers. We use the right method for each surface.

Is roof cleaning safe for my shingles or tile?

Yes. We soft wash roofs instead of blasting them with high pressure, which can damage shingles and tile. Soft washing removes the black streaks and growth while protecting your roof.

How often should I have my home cleaned in Florida?

Most Palm Coast homes benefit from a house and driveway cleaning about once a year, and a roof soft wash every couple of years. Shaded areas, pool cages and homes near trees may need it more often. Ask us about scheduling regular visits.

Will it hurt my plants, pets or pool?

We take care to protect your landscaping, furniture and screens before we start, and we rinse thoroughly when we're done. Just keep pets inside while we work.

Do I need to be home?

Not usually. As long as we can reach an outdoor water spigot and the areas being cleaned, you can go about your day. We'll send you an update when the job is done.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we give free, no-obligation quotes based on size and what needs cleaning. Bundling services in one visit is the best value. Request a quote or call (386) 264-2037.
